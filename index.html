Security and data

Built for what your agents handle.

Your customers’ conversations are not ours to keep. Plumbline is built that way from the start.

Your data

  • Opt-in. Inputs and outputs are kept only if you turn it on, and redacted first.
  • Encrypted. What an agent passes for a person to decide is stored encrypted.
  • Isolated. Every team has its own workspace; nothing is shared between them.
  • Mutual NDA with every beta team, before any data is sent.

Access and audit

  • Roles. Owner, admin, member and viewer, with invite-only accounts.
  • Scoped keys. Separate keys to send data, read it, and administer.
  • Audit log. Every decision, approval and change, with who made it.
  • Signed records. Exports and reports can be checked by anyone.

Questions

What teams ask first.

Do we have to change our code?

Not to start. If your agents already export OpenTelemetry traces, you point them at Plumbline with two settings. Stopping risky actions takes one check in front of each action, usually a few lines, and we add it with you on the setup call.

Does it replace our tracing or evals?

No. Keep the tools you use to build and debug. Plumbline answers a different question: did the agent actually get the job done in production, and should this action go ahead.

What do you store, and where?

Run records (status, timing, cost, steps) by default. Inputs and outputs only if you turn that on, redacted and encrypted. The beta is hosted in the US, with each team in its own workspace.

What if Plumbline is unreachable when an agent asks?

You decide per action: wait (the safe default for refunds and anything irreversible) or go ahead. Either way, the agent gets an answer within the time you set.

Why the NDA?

You will show us real agent behaviour, and we will show you unreleased product. A short mutual NDA protects both, and takes a few minutes to e-sign.

Know your agent did the job.

A few teams at a time, set up with the founder. Bring one agent; leave with the failures nobody saw.
